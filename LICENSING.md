# Licensing and ownership

The MIT license in LICENSE covers original University-owned WHIMC software authorized for release under MIT. Separately owned contributions retain their applicable terms. Bundled dependencies, third-party code, learner records, datasets, model weights, maps and artwork require their own license or permission.

For software resulting from University of Illinois sponsored research, the University normally owns the resulting rights, subject to the written award terms. The principal investigator can identify the applicable award, collaborating institutions and any separately owned contributions. Before releasing sponsored software under an open-source license, notify the Urbana-Champaign Office of Technology Management (OTM) at otm@illinois.edu and resolve any award or third-party restrictions.

For source files confirmed to be University-owned, use the applicable copyright year and name The Board of Trustees of the University of Illinois in the header. Identify the applicable license and include its text in the distribution. Preserve existing authorship credits, third-party copyright notices and license terms.

[Board General Rules, Article III, sections 4 and 7(q)](https://www.bot.uillinois.edu/governance/general_rules) · [Illinois OTM open-source guidance](https://otm.illinois.edu/researchers-innovators/transferring-research-beyond-academia/licensing-intellectual-property/open)
